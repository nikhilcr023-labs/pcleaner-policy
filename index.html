Privacy Policy for PCleaner

Application: PCleaner • Effective Date: September 11, 2026 • Last Updated: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PCleaner ("we," "our," or "us"). We are strongly committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how PCleaner handles information when you use our mobile application across Google Android devices.

Strict Privacy Commitment: PCleaner is engineered as a local-first utility. We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII), device contacts, media files, or user communications. All cleaning, scanning, and cache calculations occur 100% locally on your device.

2. Information We Process on Your Device

PCleaner only accesses system metadata strictly required to perform device cleaning, memory defragmentation, and storage optimization:

  • RAM & Memory Statistics: Total system RAM, currently available memory, and high-memory background process counts via Android's native ActivityManager to calculate optimization gains.
  • Storage & Cache Metadata: Internal storage capacities, residual file paths, cache folders, and obsolete package installers (.apk leftovers) to compute reclaimable space.
  • Application Package Identifiers: Publicly queryable app package names strictly used to measure temporary cache size and display installed application memory usage locally in the UI.

None of this system metadata is ever transmitted to external servers or third parties. It remains strictly within your device's runtime sandboxed memory.

3. Android Permissions & Technical Rationale

To adhere to Google Play Developer Policy guidelines on Least-Privilege Permissions, PCleaner requests only permissions necessary for core cleaner functionality:

Permission Type Purpose & Compliance Rationale
KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES Normal (Install-time) Allows PCleaner to release volatile memory allocated to dormant background applications when you tap "Optimize Now" or "Turbo Boost".
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Used strictly to verify Google Play subscriptions, process in-app billing tokens, and fetch non-personalized advertisements in the Free tier.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Optional) Optionally used to notify you when device storage reaches critical capacity or when a scheduled background cleanup completes. You can revoke this anytime in Android Settings.

4. Free vs. Pro Subscriptions & Third-Party Services

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: All financial transactions, monthly plans (₹99/mo), and annual subscriptions (₹999/yr with 3-Day Free Trial) are handled directly by the official Google Play In-App Billing API. PCleaner never receives, stores, or transmits your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.

Advertising (Free Tier Only): The Free version of PCleaner may display non-intrusive native banner advertisements supplied via certified Google Play advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may use anonymized advertising IDs subject to Google's standard privacy guidelines.

100% Ad-Free Pro Tier: When you subscribe to PCleaner PRO, all advertising code and tracking networks are completely deactivated, guaranteeing a 100% ad-free, telemetry-free experience.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Because PCleaner does not maintain remote user accounts or upload user data to cloud databases, we hold zero user data on remote servers. Any operational preferences (e.g., subscription state, guard toggles) are stored strictly on your physical device using Android SharedPreferences / Jetpack Room, and are immediately deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

PCleaner is a general utility tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Security Safeguards

We prioritize device security by strictly adhering to modern Android security architectures:

  • Strict adherence to Android Scoped Storage and sandboxing guidelines.
  • Zero dynamic code loading (DCL) from external repositories.
  • All cleaning algorithms operate non-destructively, targeting only temporary cache directories without affecting personal photos, documents, or operating system integrity.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to comply with legal regulations or changes in our app features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date.
