Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide our device optimization and cleaning services, our app requests specific permissions and accesses certain on-device data. All optimization processing occurs locally on your device.

  • Storage Access (Photos, Media, and Files): We request read and write access to your device's storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) strictly to identify and remove junk files, cache, and duplicate files (via our Deep Shredder tool). We do not upload your personal files to any external servers.
  • Installed Applications & Battery Usage: To provide the Battery Usage Monitor and Turbo RAM Engine features, we query the list of installed applications and use the KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES permission to hibernate power-draining background apps. We do not harvest, track, or transmit your list of installed apps or your app usage history off your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or serve personalized content:

  • Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and general location data to serve personalized ads and analyze ad performance.
  • Google Play Billing: For our Pro Subscriptions, payment processing is handled securely by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card or payment details.

3. Data Retention and Security

Because the core functions of this app (cleaning, battery optimization, RAM management) occur locally on your device, we do not store your personal files or device metrics on our servers. Any data collected by third-party services (like AdMob) is subject to their respective retention policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us at the support email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
