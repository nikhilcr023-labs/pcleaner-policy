Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Welcome to Auto PCleaner ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Auto PCleaner is designed with your privacy in mind. Most device optimization and cleaning operations occur locally on your device. We may collect or access the following types of information:

  • Device & Storage Information: Information about device storage, running apps, and cache directories required to perform cleaning, junk removal, duplicate file scanning, and startup management.
  • Accessibility Service Data: If enabled by you, our Accessibility Service operates locally on your device solely to automate cache clearing and background process optimization requested by you. We do not collect, store, or transmit any sensitive personal data or keystrokes via accessibility services.
  • Usage & Performance Statistics: Diagnostic logs and app performance data to help us fix bugs and improve application stability.
  • Advertising Identifiers: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use advertising IDs and device information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our app's core cleaning, boosting, and battery optimization features.
  • To analyze app performance and fix technical issues.
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob to support the free availability of the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: For advertising and analytics. You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Since core cleaning functions run locally on your device, your personal files remain private and secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Auto PCleaner does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete such information immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
