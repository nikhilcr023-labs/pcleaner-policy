Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Data Processing and Storage

Our application is designed with a privacy-first approach. All core optimization, memory management, and file scanning features are performed locally on your device. We do not upload your personal files, photos, or usage statistics to external servers for processing.

2. Permissions We Request

To provide our optimization services, the app requires specific Android permissions. We only request permissions necessary for the app's functionality:

  • All Files Access (Manage External Storage): Required for the "Deep Storage & Duplicate Shredder" feature. This allows the app to scan your entire device storage to identify exact duplicate files by calculating secure MD5 byte hashes. No files are moved, uploaded, or analyzed remotely. Files are only deleted upon your explicit confirmation.
  • Usage Access (Package Usage Stats): Used to monitor battery usage and identify power-draining applications for hibernation.
  • Kill Background Processes: Used by our Turbo RAM Engine and 24/7 Automated Background Guard to halt dormant tasks and prevent memory leaks.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to verify Google Play subscriptions and to deliver advertisements in the Free version of the app.

3. Advertisements & Pro Version

The Free version of our app is ad-supported. Advertising partners may collect non-identifiable device data to serve relevant ads. Users who upgrade to the Pro Subscription enjoy an Ads-Free Experience, meaning no ad networks will be loaded and no ad-tracking data will be collected.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
